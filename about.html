Our Approach to Every Puja

Pandit Sanjeev Rai Ji believes that a puja is not only a ritual, but a spiritual experience that brings faith, positivity and harmony into a family. The preparation begins with understanding the devotee's needs, the purpose of the ceremony and the required samagri.

During the ceremony, each mantra and ritual is performed according to traditional customs. Families are guided properly so that they can take part in the puja with understanding and devotion. Special attention is given to maintaining proper procedure, auspicious timing and a calm environment.
